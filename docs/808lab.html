What AGE does

AGE is how worn out the track is. At 0 it is a clean machine.
DRIFT is the slop: pitch wanders from hit to hit and drifts slowly, decays and levels vary, hits land a few ms late.
HISS is the noise: hiss that only sounds while the drum does, fed in before the amp so it dies away with the hit.
GRIT is the damage: soft clipping, lost bits, and a dull top end.
Keys 1 to 8 select and hit a machine, space plays and stops.
