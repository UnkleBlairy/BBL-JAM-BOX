How the engine sounds

PITCH is plus or minus two octaves and resamples like an old sampler, so it changes length too.
STRETCH changes length without changing pitch, using short grains: rough on purpose. Left of centre is faster, right is slower.
START / LEN choose the part of the chunk: where it starts and how long it plays. Move START and the window moves with it.
MODE is once, loop, ping-pong or reverse. Loops hold for the gate length.
LOFI is the Amiga knob: fewer bits, a lower sample rate and no smoothing between samples.
Keys 1 to 4 select and hit a track. The keys A to K (white and black on a piano layout) play the selected track chromatically, space plays and stops.
