Samples

Pick a sound file, choose the part you want and press Write to add it to the list. Then connect the FM-1 by USB and press Upload: the list replaces everything in the FM-1's sample memory. A SAMPLE track plays slot S01 to S32 (its SLOT knob). The FM-1 keeps them when it's switched off.

Chrome or Edge (Web MIDI). Close other apps that use the FM-1 first. The sound stops while it loads. This browser keeps your list, so next time you can add to it.

WAV, MP3, OGG or FLAC.
