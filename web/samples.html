BBL JAM BOX

SAMPLES · SOUNDS INTO YOUR FM-1

1. PICK A PART

Choose a sound file, set START and END, then press WRITE to add it to the list.

WAV, MP3, OGG or FLAC.

To find a loop: tick LOOP, press PLAY and move START and END as it plays. For tiny moves, click a slider and use the arrow keys.

2. UPLOAD THE LIST

Connect the FM-1 by USB and press UPLOAD. The list replaces everything in the FM-1's sample memory. A SAMPLE track plays slots S01 to S32 (its SLOT knob), and the FM-1 keeps them when it's switched off.

MEMORY

    Chrome or Edge only. Close other apps that use the FM-1 first. The sound stops while it loads. This browser keeps your list, so next time you can add to it.
