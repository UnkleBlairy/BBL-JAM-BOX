FM-1 Groovebox

A free beta firmware that turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a 6-track groovebox: FM drums and synth, 808 drums and a sampler.

BETA: flash at your own risk This is test firmware. It may have bugs. Before you start, download the official FM-1 V15 firmware from M-VAVE so you can go back to it.
1. Install the firmwareConnect the FM-1 by USB, open in Chrome or Edge 2. Load samplesPut your own sounds on the FM-1 (after installing)

You need a computer with Chrome or Edge. Close any other music apps that use the FM-1 first. New to this? Read the step-by-step guide (plain text) first: it explains how to go back to the official firmware.
