WHAT IS THIS?

The same BBL JAM BOX firmware the M-VAVE FM-1 runs, running in your browser: the same sounds, screen, sequencer and saves. Click the panel, use your computer keyboard, or plug in a MIDI keyboard / controller. Your sounds, kits and last jam are kept in this browser. Works best in Chrome or Edge.

COMPUTER KEYBOARD

Laid out like the FM-1: the two rows of buttons on the number row and Q row, the keys like a piano on the Z and A rows (W = white key, B = black key; in SEQ mode B1-B6 pick the tracks, B7 the FX track, the black keys 8-11 the pages).

MIDI

Plug in a MIDI keyboard or controller, then press SWITCH ON (allow MIDI when the browser asks). Pick it under CONTROLLER (or ALL). Its keys play notes on the selected track, like the FM-1's keys. MIDI LEARN: press it, click a knob on the panel (KNOB 1-4, SELECT, PRESETS, ALGORITHM or MASTER), then move a control on your controller. Do it again for each knob you want. The emulator remembers them.

SAMPLES

Pick a sound file, choose the part you want and press Write to add it to the list. Then press Upload: the list replaces everything in the FM-1's sample memory. A SAMPLE track plays slot S01 to S32 (its SLOT knob).

WAV, MP3, OGG or FLAC.
